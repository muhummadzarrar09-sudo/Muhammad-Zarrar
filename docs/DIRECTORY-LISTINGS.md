# Directory listings pack

Everything needed to list Zarrar.Solutions on the directories that actually
send buyers — copy, categories, pricing fields, and a tracker.

**Why this matters:** GoodFirms alone lists 1,078 software companies in
Pakistan with a median advertised rate of $37/hour, and DesignRush ranks
agencies by hourly rate, minimum budget and portfolio count. Buyers shortlist
from those two screens before they ever see a website. Right now we are
invisible on both.

**The one rule:** no invented numbers, no invented clients, no "50+ projects".
Directories verify. A profile that survives verification is worth more than
three that don't, and this studio's entire pitch is that the numbers are real.

## 2. Platform by platform

### A. GoodFirms — highest value, free
- 1,078 Pakistani software companies listed; reviews drive ranking.
- **Steps:** create a company profile → verify by email/phone → complete every
  field (incomplete profiles rank below complete ones) → add services with the
  prices above → add portfolio items → request reviews from real clients as
  soon as there is one.
- **Do first:** the "focus areas" and "portfolio" fields. They are what the
  filter UI actually uses.

### B. Clutch — highest trust signal, slowest
- Listing requires client reviews to rank; approval can take weeks.
- **Steps:** claim/create profile → submit for review → line up 2 real clients
  (even small engagements) who will take a 15-minute verification call.
- **Do not** list until at least one review is possible. An empty Clutch
  profile ranks below no profile.

### C. DesignRush — fast, ranks by rate and portfolio count
- Adds hourly rate, minimum budget and portfolio count to the card.
- **Steps:** submit the agency listing, add 3 portfolio entries (the honest
  table above), keep minimum budget at $1,000–$4,999.

### D. Google Business Profile — do this before any directory
- Category: **Website designer** (+ secondary: Internet marketing service).
- Service area: Rawalpindi, Islamabad. Hours, phone, and the same name as the
  site. Add the site URL with a tracked UTM if desired.
- Post the field notes there monthly; a profile that posts outranks one that
  doesn't.

### E. Bing Places — 10 minutes, near-zero competition
- Imports from Google Business Profile in most cases.

### F. LinkedIn company page
- Use the medium bio verbatim. Pin the audit offer. This is where B2B buyers
  check whether a studio is real.

### G. Pakistani directories
- Local business directories reward consistent NAP (name/address/phone). Use
  the exact contact block above — no variations, not even "+92 0333…".

## 4. What not to do

- No "50+ projects delivered", no "99% client satisfaction", no invented
  awards. Every one of those is a verification risk.
- No stock team photos. A one-person studio that looks like a 40-person agency
  reads as a lie the moment the client video-calls.
- No hourly rate that undercuts the audit fee. Being the cheapest listing is
  the competitor's strategy, and it loses to evidence every time.
- Don't copy the competitor bios. Every profile with the same paragraph is how
  directories start flagging duplicates.
